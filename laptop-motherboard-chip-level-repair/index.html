When the problem is on the motherboard

A new charger, battery or screen does not help if the fault is on the motherboard. Signs that point to the board include a laptop that is completely dead, does not charge even with a working charger, or shows no display while the fan spins.

  • No power or dead laptop
  • Not charging or charging not detected
  • No display although the laptop seems to start
  • Laptop switching off by itself
  • BIOS-related problems

These symptoms can also come from simpler causes, so we check the outside parts first and only then go to board level.

FAQ

Motherboard and chip-level repair: common questions

What is chip-level laptop repair?

It means finding and repairing the faulty component on the motherboard, instead of treating the whole board as one part. It suits faults in the power, charging or display circuits, depending on the damage.

Is a repair cheaper than replacing the motherboard?

It can be, but it depends on the fault and the laptop model. That is why we diagnose first and give you an estimate before any work begins.

Can every motherboard be repaired?

No. Some boards are too badly damaged or the parts are not available. After diagnosis we tell you honestly whether repair is suitable.

Do you charge for diagnosis?

No. Diagnosis and repair estimate are free.

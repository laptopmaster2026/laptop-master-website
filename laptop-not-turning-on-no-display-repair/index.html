Quick checks before you visit

These safe checks solve some problems and help us diagnose the rest:

  • Check that the charger light or the laptop charging light comes on. Try another wall socket.
  • Unplug USB drives, printers and other accessories, then try again.
  • Disconnect the charger, hold the power button for about 30 seconds, then reconnect and start.
  • If the laptop starts but the screen is black, try an external monitor through HDMI.

If none of this works, do not keep pressing the power button or open the laptop yourself. Bring it in and we will find the real fault.

No power and no display: common questions

Why is my laptop not turning on?

Common causes are a faulty charger or charging port, a drained or failed battery, a problem with RAM, or a fault on the motherboard. A diagnosis is needed to know which one it is.

My laptop turns on but the screen stays black. What now?

Connect an external monitor if you can. If the external screen works, the problem is likely the laptop display or its cable. If it does not, the fault may be with graphics or the motherboard.
